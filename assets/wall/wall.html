592 of 753 name a concrete signal model — 543 say so in the abstract. A model-specific search is a question that does not change; only the dataset does. The rest: 27 model-agnostic, 134 SM-process searches.
Run 1 was re-run: 191 of 219 model-specific searches have an audited Run-2 successor. By hand, that took the collaboration a decade. 13 never came back.
Run 2 is waiting: 351 of 369 model-specific searches have no Run-3 update yet. The decade starts over — unless the by-hand part stops being by hand.
That is the job to hand to agents: re-run the wall every time the dataset doubles — and give the people back to the techniques that need building. The next forty minutes are the evidence that they can.
